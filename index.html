what I'm about.

I grew up in California’s Central Valley in communities where technology careers rarely felt visible or accessible. My path into engineering began through public education, mentorship, and community work — experiences that shaped how I think about technology today. I believe software is most meaningful when it helps people navigate systems more effectively, creates access to opportunity, and strengthens communities rather than simply optimizing engagement or growth metrics. My work today focuses on building systems that are practical, scalable, and grounded in real human needs — from civic and nonprofit platforms to AI-assisted operational tools and modern product infrastructure.
